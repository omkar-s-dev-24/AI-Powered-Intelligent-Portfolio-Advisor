Metric Explanations
Beta (Market Sensitivity)
Beta measures how much the stock moves compared to the market.
• Beta > 1.5: Highly Volatile (High Risk).
• Beta < 1.0: Stable/Defensive (Low Risk).
Max Drawdown
The maximum observed loss from a peak to a trough. It answers the question: "If I bought at the absolute worst time, how much would I have lost?"
Sharpe Ratio
Measures return per unit of risk.
• > 1.0: Good investment.
• < 0: The return is not worth the risk (or losing money).
Annual Volatility
The standard deviation of returns. Higher volatility means the price swings wildly, increasing the chance of loss.
