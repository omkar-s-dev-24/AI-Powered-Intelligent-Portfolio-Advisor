Scoring Logic

The "AI Confidence Score" (0-100) is a weighted aggregate of four distinct modules:

Fundamental Analysis (ROE, P/E) 35%
Technical Trend (ML Prediction) 25%
Sentiment Analysis (News NLP) 25%
Risk Analysis (Volatility) 15%
